② “Who answers for leelagarden.com.np?” The registry: “ask Cloudflare (eugene and kara)”.

③ “What is the A record?” Cloudflare: “169.58.157.28”.

① The browser asks a resolver. ④ The resolver hands back 169.58.157.28 and remembers it for the TTL, so the next visit skips the lookup.

In Present mode click to reveal the steps. 1: the browser asks a resolver. 2: the resolver asks the .np registry who is in charge of the name; the registry points to Cloudflare's nameservers eugene and kara. 3: the resolver asks Cloudflare for the A record and gets 169.58.157.28. 4: the answer goes back to the browser and is cached. All of this usually takes a few milliseconds, and most visits skip it entirely because the answer is already cached.
