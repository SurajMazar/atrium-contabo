Putting it together

A first visit, from Enter to photos

1 DNS lookup

A cached answer, or a quick ask to Cloudflare: 169.58.157.28.

2 Knock on the door

Connect to port 443. The firewall lets it through.

3 Handshake

nginx shows the certificate; the browser checks it and shows the padlock.

4 Route by name

nginx matches leelagarden.com.np and passes it to the renderer.

5 Cache or build

Cached? Sent at once. If not: renderer, API, Postgres, then cached.

6 HTML arrives

The browser then fetches CSS, scripts and fonts from the same server.

7 Photos from R2

Images come straight from Cloudflare R2 storage, so they never load your VPS.

8 Contact form

reCAPTCHA gives a token; the API checks it with Google, then saves the message.

This is the full sequence for someone visiting for the first time. Steps 1 to 6 bring the page; most of them take a few milliseconds each. Step 7 is why you set up R2: photos are the heaviest part of a page, and they are served by Cloudflare from media.sites.surajthapa247.com.np, not by your 7-dollar VPS. Step 8 only happens when someone submits the contact form.
