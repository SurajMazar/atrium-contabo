Connecting the next client's domain

1

In Atrium: Website, Domains, Add domain (for example clientsite.com).

2

In the client's DNS: the A record (@ to 169.58.157.28) and the TXT record Atrium shows. Grey cloud.

3

Wait a few minutes, then click Verify until it shows Active and Valid.

4

On the server: add the nginx rules and issue the certificate with certbot. Ask me, or we script it.

5

Test: open https://clientsite.com in a private window and check the padlock.

6

Want www too? Add a www record and include it in the certificate; nginx redirects it to the root.

Steps 1 to 3 happen in dashboards and need no server access. Step 4 is the manual one today, highlighted in orange: until certificates are automated, every new client domain needs a server change. Step 5: a private window avoids your browser's own cache. If Verify keeps failing, check the records with an online DNS lookup tool against 1.1.1.1 and 8.8.8.8 first.
